# Problem 5 – Capacitor: energy and force

Given parallel plate capacitor:

$$
S=0.02\,m^2
$$

$$
d=5\,mm=0.005\,m
$$

$$
U=500\,V
$$

Vacuum permittivity:

$$
\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot 10^{-12}\,F/m
$$

---

## 1. Capacitance

For a parallel plate capacitor:

$$
C=\varepsilon_0\frac{S}{d}
$$

Substitute:

$$
C=8.85\cdot 10^{-12}\frac{0.02}{0.005}
$$

$$
C=8.85\cdot 10^{-12}\cdot 4
$$

$$
C=3.54\cdot 10^{-11}\,F
$$

---

## 2. Energy stored in capacitor

Energy:

$$
W=\frac12CU^2
$$

$$
W=\frac12(3.54\cdot 10^{-11})(500)^2
$$

$$
W=0.5\cdot 3.54\cdot 10^{-11}\cdot 250000
$$

$$
W=4.425\cdot 10^{-6}\,J
$$

---

## 3. Electric field intensity

For parallel plates:

$$
E=\frac{U}{d}
$$

$$
E=\frac{500}{0.005}
$$

$$
E=100000\,V/m
$$

---

## 4. Field energy density

Energy density:

$$
u=\frac12\varepsilon_0E^2
$$

$$
u=\frac12(8.85\cdot 10^{-12})(100000)^2
$$

$$
u=0.04425\,J/m^3
$$

---

## 5. Force of attraction between plates

Pressure:

$$
p=\frac12\varepsilon_0E^2
$$

Force:

$$
F=pS
$$

$$
F=0.04425\cdot 0.02
$$

$$
F=8.85\cdot 10^{-4}\,N
$$

---

## Final results

Capacitance:

$$
C=3.54\cdot 10^{-11}\,F
$$

Energy:

$$
W=4.425\cdot 10^{-6}\,J
$$

Electric field:

$$
E=100000\,V/m
$$

Energy density:

$$
u=0.04425\,J/m^3
$$

Force:

$$
F=8.85\cdot 10^{-4}\,N
$$

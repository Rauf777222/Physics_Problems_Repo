# Problem 1 – Lorentz force

Given:

$$
\vec B=(0,0,1)\,T
$$

$$
\vec v=(2,3,0)\,m/s
$$

$$
q=1\,mC=10^{-3}C
$$

---

## 1. Lorentz force

Magnetic Lorentz force:

$$
\vec F=q(\vec v\times \vec B)
$$

Compute cross product:

$$
\vec v\times \vec B=
(2,3,0)\times(0,0,1)
$$

$$
\vec v\times \vec B=(3,-2,0)
$$

Thus:

$$
\vec F=10^{-3}(3,-2,0)
$$

$$
\vec F=(0.003,-0.002,0)\,N
$$

---

## 2. Magnitude of force

$$
|\vec F|=qvB
$$

Since \(v\perp B\):

$$
v=\sqrt{2^2+3^2}=\sqrt{13}
$$

$$
|\vec F|=10^{-3}\sqrt{13}
$$

$$
|\vec F|\approx 3.61\cdot 10^{-3}\,N
$$

---

## 3. Magnetic force does no work

Power:

$$
P=\vec F\cdot \vec v
$$

Since \(\vec F\perp \vec v\):

$$
P=0
$$

So magnetic force changes direction of velocity, but not kinetic energy.

---

## 4. Radius of trajectory

For circular motion:

$$
qvB=\frac{mv^2}{r}
$$

So:

$$
r=\frac{mv}{qB}
$$

Given:

$$
m=0.01\,kg
$$

$$
r=\frac{0.01\sqrt{13}}{10^{-3}\cdot 1}
$$

$$
r\approx 36.06\,m
$$

---

## 5. If \(B\) is doubled

$$
r=\frac{mv}{qB}
$$

If \(B\) doubles, radius becomes half.

---

## Final results

$$
\vec F=(0.003,-0.002,0)\,N
$$

$$
|\vec F|\approx 3.61\cdot 10^{-3}\,N
$$

Magnetic force does no work.

$$
r\approx 36.06\,m
$$

If \(B\) doubles, \(r\) becomes two times smaller.

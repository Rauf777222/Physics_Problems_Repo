# Problem 2 – Velocity selection

Given crossed fields:

$$
\vec E=(0,E,0)
$$

$$
\vec B=(0,0,B)
$$

---

## 1. Condition for rectilinear motion

The total Lorentz force is:

$$
\vec F=q(\vec E+\vec v\times \vec B)
$$

For straight motion without deflection:

$$
\vec F=0
$$

So:

$$
\vec E+\vec v\times \vec B=0
$$

Assume the particle moves along the \(x\)-axis:

$$
\vec v=(v,0,0)
$$

Then:

$$
\vec v\times \vec B=(v,0,0)\times(0,0,B)
$$

$$
\vec v\times \vec B=(0,-vB,0)
$$

Thus:

$$
(0,E,0)+(0,-vB,0)=(0,0,0)
$$

So:

$$
E-vB=0

# Problem 3 – Magnetic moment of a loop

A conducting loop has:

- number of turns \(N\),
- area \(S\),
- current \(I\),
- uniform magnetic field \(\vec B\).

---

## 1. Magnetic moment

The magnetic moment of a current loop is:

$$
\vec \mu = N I S \hat n
$$

where \(\hat n\) is the unit vector perpendicular to the surface of the loop.

The magnitude is:

$$
\mu = NIS
$$

---

## 2. Torque on the loop

A magnetic dipole in a magnetic field experiences torque:

$$
\vec M=\vec \mu \times \vec B
$$

The magnitude is:

$$
M=\mu B\sin\theta
$$

where \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\vec \mu\) and \(\vec B\).

---

## 3. Angle for maximum torque

Torque is maximum when:

$$
\sin\theta=1
$$

So:

$$
\theta=90^\circ
$$

Thus:

$$
M_{\max}=\mu B=NISB
$$

---

## 4. Potential energy

The potential energy of a magnetic dipole is:

$$
U=-\vec \mu\cdot \vec B
$$

So:

$$
U=-\mu B\cos\theta
$$

---

## 5. Stable and unstable positions

Stable position occurs when potential energy is minimum.

Since

$$
U=-\mu B\cos\theta
$$

minimum occurs when:

$$
\theta=0^\circ
$$

So \(\vec \mu\) is parallel to \(\vec B\).

Unstable position occurs when:

$$
\theta=180^\circ
$$

So \(\vec \mu\) is opposite to \(\vec B\).

---

## Final results

Magnetic moment:

$$
\vec \mu=NIS\hat n
$$

Torque:

$$
\vec M=\vec \mu\times \vec B
$$

Torque magnitude:

$$
M=NISB\sin\theta
$$

Potential energy:

$$
U=-NISB\cos\theta
$$

Maximum torque:

$$
\theta=90^\circ
$$

Stable position:

$$
\theta=0^\circ
$$

Unstable position:

$$
\theta=180^\circ
$$

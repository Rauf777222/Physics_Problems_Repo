# Problem 4 – Rotating loop (induction)

A loop with area \(S\) and \(N\) turns rotates in a uniform magnetic field \(\vec B\) with angular velocity \(\omega\).

---

## 1. Magnetic flux

Magnetic flux through one turn is:

$$
\Phi_1(t)=BS\cos(\omega t)
$$

For \(N\) turns:

$$
\Phi(t)=NBS\cos(\omega t)
$$

---

## 2. Induced EMF

By Faraday's law:

$$
\mathcal E(t)=-\frac{d\Phi}{dt}
$$

Differentiate:

$$
\frac{d\Phi}{dt}=-NBS\omega\sin(\omega t)
$$

Thus:

$$
\mathcal E(t)=NBS\omega\sin(\omega t)
$$

---

## 3. Amplitude of EMF

The amplitude is:

$$
\mathcal E_0=NBS\omega
$$

---

## 4. Dependence on angular velocity

Since:

$$
\mathcal E_0=NBS\omega
$$

the amplitude is directly proportional to \(\omega\).

If \(\omega\) doubles, the EMF amplitude also doubles.

---

## 5. Interpretation

As the loop rotates, the magnetic flux changes in time.

Changing magnetic flux produces induced voltage.

This is the basic mechanism of an electric generator.

---

## Final results

Flux:

$$
\Phi(t)=NBS\cos(\omega t)
$$

Induced EMF:

$$
\mathcal E(t)=NBS\omega\sin(\omega t)
$$

Amplitude:

$$
\mathcal E_0=NBS\omega
$$

# Problem 4 – Voltage and current through \(R_3\)

Given:

$$
R_1=20\Omega
$$

$$
R_2=60\Omega
$$

$$
R_3=30\Omega
$$

$$
R_4=10\Omega
$$

$$
U=200V
$$

---

## 1. Parallel part

Resistors \(R_2\) and \(R_3\) are connected in parallel:

$$
R_{23}=\frac{R_2R_3}{R_2+R_3}
$$

$$
R_{23}=\frac{60\cdot 30}{60+30}
$$

$$
R_{23}=\frac{1800}{90}=20\Omega
$$

---

## 2. Total resistance

The circuit is:

$$
R_1 + R_{23} + R_4
$$

So:

$$
R_{\text{eq}}=20+20+10
$$

$$
R_{\text{eq}}=50\Omega
$$

---

## 3. Total current

Using Ohm's law:

$$
I=\frac{U}{R_{\text{eq}}}
$$

$$
I=\frac{200}{50}
$$

$$
I=4A
$$

---

## 4. Voltage across \(R_3\)

Since \(R_2\) and \(R_3\) are parallel, they have the same voltage.

Voltage across the parallel part:

$$
U_{23}=IR_{23}
$$

$$
U_{23}=4\cdot 20
$$

$$
U_{23}=80V
$$

Therefore:

$$
U_3=80V
$$

---

## 5. Current through \(R_3\)

$$
I_3=\frac{U_3}{R_3}
$$

$$
I_3=\frac{80}{30}
$$

$$
I_3=\frac{8}{3}A
$$

$$
I_3\approx 2.67A
$$

---

## Final results

Voltage across \(R_3\):

$$
U_3=80V
$$

Current through \(R_3\):

$$
I_3=\frac{8}{3}A\approx 2.67A
$$

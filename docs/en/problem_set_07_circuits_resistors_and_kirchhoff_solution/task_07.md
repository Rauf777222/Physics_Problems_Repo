# Problem 7 – Equivalent resistance and potential difference between A and B

Given:

$$
U=3V
$$

Top branch:

$$
6\Omega + 4\Omega
$$

Bottom branch:

$$
3\Omega + 12\Omega
$$

---

## 1. Resistance of the top branch

$$
R_{\text{top}}=6+4
$$

$$
R_{\text{top}}=10\Omega
$$

---

## 2. Resistance of the bottom branch

$$
R_{\text{bottom}}=3+12
$$

$$
R_{\text{bottom}}=15\Omega
$$

---

## 3. Equivalent resistance

The two branches are connected in parallel:

$$
R_{\text{eq}}=\frac{R_{\text{top}}R_{\text{bottom}}}{R_{\text{top}}+R_{\text{bottom}}}
$$

$$
R_{\text{eq}}=\frac{10\cdot 15}{10+15}
$$

$$
R_{\text{eq}}=\frac{150}{25}=6\Omega
$$

---

## 4. Total current

$$
I=\frac{U}{R_{\text{eq}}}
$$

$$
I=\frac{3}{6}
$$

$$
I=0.5A
$$

---

## 5. Branch currents

Top branch:

$$
I_{\text{top}}=\frac{3}{10}
$$

$$
I_{\text{top}}=0.3A
$$

Bottom branch:

$$
I_{\text{bottom}}=\frac{3}{15}
$$

$$
I_{\text{bottom}}=0.2A
$$

---

## 6. Potential difference between A and B

The voltage drops in each branch are different.

Voltage drop across the \(6\Omega\) resistor:

$$
U_6=0.3\cdot 6=1.8V
$$

Voltage drop across the \(3\Omega\) resistor:

$$
U_3=0.2\cdot 3=0.6V
$$

The difference between points \(A\) and \(B\) has magnitude:

$$
|U_{AB}|=1.2V
$$

Depending on the chosen polarity of the source, the sign may be positive or negative.

---

## Final results

Equivalent resistance:

$$
R_{\text{eq}}=6\Omega
$$

Total current:

$$
I=0.5A
$$

Top branch current:

$$
I_{\text{top}}=0.3A
$$

Bottom branch current:

$$
I_{\text{bottom}}=0.2A
$$

Potential difference magnitude:

$$
|U_{AB}|=1.2V
$$

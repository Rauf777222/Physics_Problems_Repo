# Problem 8 – Current through the ammeter

Given:

$$
\mathcal E_1=4.5V
$$

$$
\mathcal E_2=1.5V
$$

$$
r_w=1\Omega
$$

$$
R_1=20\Omega
$$

$$
R_2=40\Omega
$$

$$
R_3=60\Omega
$$

The circuit is one series loop.

---

## 1. Total resistance

All resistances are in series:

$$
R_{\text{total}}=R_1+R_2+R_3+r_{w1}+r_{w2}
$$

$$
R_{\text{total}}=20+40+60+1+1
$$

$$
R_{\text{total}}=122\Omega
$$

---

## 2. Net electromotive force

From the figure, the sources are connected oppositely.

Therefore:

$$
\mathcal E_{\text{net}}=\mathcal E_1-\mathcal E_2
$$

$$
\mathcal E_{\text{net}}=4.5-1.5
$$

$$
\mathcal E_{\text{net}}=3.0V
$$

---

## 3. Current through the circuit

Using Ohm's law:

$$
I=\frac{\mathcal E_{\text{net}}}{R_{\text{total}}}
$$

$$
I=\frac{3.0}{122}
$$

$$
I\approx 0.0246A
$$

Since the ammeter is in series, it measures the same current.

---

## Final result

Current through the ammeter:

$$
I_A\approx 0.0246A
$$

or

$$
I_A\approx 24.6mA
$$
